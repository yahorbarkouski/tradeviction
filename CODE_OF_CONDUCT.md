# Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, and discussions, is expected to be respectful and constructive.

Be kind. Assume good faith. Disagree about the code, not the person. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and sustained disruption are not welcome here, and the maintainers will remove people who engage in them.

If something happens that you want the maintainers to know about, open a private report through
[GitHub](https://github.com/yahorbarkouski/tradeviction/security/advisories/new) or contact a maintainer directly.

This document is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
